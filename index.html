Common questions

Straight answers, for people and for search.

What does Threshold Technologies do?

Threshold is an enterprise IT engineering company. We build AI and data platforms, connect enterprise systems, and fine-tune AI models on industry-specific data. We also operate Threshold Relay, a secure file transfer platform.

What is Threshold Relay?

Threshold Relay is a secure file transfer platform that moves data across organizational and technology domains, with encryption, access controls, and a full audit trail on every transfer.

What is domain-specific AI fine-tuning?

It's the process of adapting a general-purpose AI model to an organization's own data, terminology, and regulatory constraints, so it performs reliably on industry-specific tasks rather than generic ones.

Who does Threshold work with?

Mid-size to large enterprises in regulated or systems-heavy industries — financial services, healthcare, logistics, and the public sector.
